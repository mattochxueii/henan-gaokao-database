# 河南新高考全国Ⅰ卷风格模拟卷　英语 · 听力朗读稿

监考用。两节材料均读两遍。第一节每段后留10秒。第二节每组读前按每题5秒读题，读后按每题5秒作答。

## Text 1

W: I thought you'd cycle to work today.

M: I would have, but the repair shop won't have my bike ready until Friday. I caught the bus.

## Text 2

M: Did they move the concert because it was going to rain?

W: No. Too many people booked for the small hall. The entrance time hasn't changed.

## Text 3

W: Could you turn the light down? I can see the numbers on the screen, but the lamp is reflected in it.

M: I'll move it behind you. I still need some light to take notes.

## Text 4

M: The library doors close at six, but the return slot is open all evening. You can drop the novel there after training.

W: Good. I won't have to carry it to school tomorrow.

## Text 5

W: Have you chosen a photo for the cover? The one from the river is beautiful.

M: I've used the one of the empty workshop. You can tell at a glance what the article is about.

## Text 6

W: Welcome to the museum. Your group is early, so you can leave your coats here. The guide is delayed by twenty minutes.

M: Could we see the map gallery in the meantime?

W: Certainly. Please stay on the ground floor. School groups may go upstairs only with their guides.

M: I'll tell the students. We'll meet back here when she arrives.

## Text 7

M: The first trial of our tool box went well, but several return forms came back wet.

W: Did you put the box by the outside gate?

M: Yes. People can reach it when the office is closed. I don't want to lose that advantage.

W: Keep the box there, then. Put the instructions on the lid and ask people to record returns on their phones. I can put the new notice up this Thursday, before the second trial starts.

## Text 8

W: I'm calling about my Saturday shift at the garden. My train gets in at eleven, and the shift starts at ten.

M: Don't try to rush. The morning volunteers label pots, but we need someone to welcome visitors after lunch.

W: That works. I've given tours there before. Do I need to bring the printed map?

M: We have new maps at the gate. Please collect one there. Your old map doesn't show the new exit.

## Text 9

M: Last year your class built small boats for the school exhibition. Why did you change the task this year?

W: Visitors went straight to the fastest boat and rarely asked how it worked. This time each group places an early design beside the final model.

M: What if students are embarrassed by their first design?

W: We asked them to choose a failure they can explain. One group kept the boat that sank when a weight was placed near the edge.

M: And what's your role during the exhibition?

W: I'll introduce the method, but the students will answer visitors' questions.

## Text 10

M: This is a notice for tomorrow's river walk. The weather should be dry, but last night's storm damaged the path beyond the old bridge. We will therefore start at the usual gate and stop at the bridge. The walk will take forty minutes instead of an hour. Bring the same water and shoes you planned to bring. At the bridge, anyone who wants to learn about the repairs can join a ten-minute talk by our ranger. If you are catching the twelve o'clock bus, leave before the talk; there is no need to check out with the group. The photographs from the walk will be posted on the park website on Monday.
